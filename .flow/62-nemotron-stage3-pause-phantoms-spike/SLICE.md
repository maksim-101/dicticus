# 62 nemotron-stage3-pause-phantoms-spike (quick)

## Brief

**Outcome.** The owner can decide, from counted results, whether Nemotron 3.5 Transcribe is safe to offer as an opt-in macOS engine given how the owner dictates: the number of dictations in which it invents words in or after a thinking pause, and the number in which it loses speech that follows a pause, measured on real dictations with pauses and on the padded test clips, next to Whisper's figures on the same audio. Spike 020 then ends with a verdict (PASS or FAIL by a rule frozen before the first decode) in place of an open Stage 3.

**Problem.** Spike 020 is two thirds done and its last question is the one that decides it. Stage 1 passed (runs on Metal, 19.5 GB footprint, 1.2 s for a 10 s clip). Stage 2 passed (German 3.74 against Whisper's 5.35 word errors per 100 words, English a tie, dictionary terms help English), but under the plain prompt one of 114 dictations (136 s long) came back as 198 invented words. Nothing has been measured on pauses, and the owner holds the hotkey through pauses of one to several seconds at the start, in the middle and at the end of a dictation. Checked on 2026-11-14 for this brief:

- The October pause clips (quick 261018-940) cover trailing pauses only: 118 clips with 3 to 8 s of room tone appended, at two noise levels. They hold no leading and no interior pause. Whisper produced 0 phantoms on the 82 single-window clips of that set while it does produce phantoms live, so a zero there says little by itself.
- Since slice 60 (this morning) the debug build keeps each dictation's audio. 33 recordings exist (739 s in total, median 18 s, longest 74 s); 28 of them hold a pause of 1 s or more: 10 leading, 20 interior, 7 trailing, the longest 11 s, 15 s and 18 s. These are the pauses the usage fact describes, and the first sample on which a pause phantom can show.
- The model files (19.7 GB and 1.7 GB), the runtime build (`nemo-speech 0.2.0` starts) and the Stage 2 decodes of the unpadded clips (arms N0 and N2) are on disk.

**For whom.** The owner, deciding whether a later slice builds the opt-in engine for Macs with 32 GB or more; nobody else uses the result.

**Done criteria.**

1. `[check]` `new`: the rule is frozen before any Stage 3 decode exists: sample lists (file hashes), arms, scoring rules and the PASS/FAIL table, hash-locked as in Stage 2, and the scorer refuses to run when a hash differs. Observed by the file times (the lock file is older than the first decode output) and by the scorer's hash check.
2. `[check]` `new`: the real-recording sample is decoded by Nemotron in two arms, N0 (the runtime's plain prompt) and N2 (the same prompt plus the 182 dictionary terms frozen in Stage 2, which is what a product would send), with the language each dictation's debug record names. The sample is every retained recording that exists at the freeze, copied to the spike's cache outside the repo so the seven-day purge cannot change it. Compared word by word with the Whisper text the app produced for the same audio (the record's raw transcript). Reported per arm, as counts: generation loops (Stage 2's rule: more than twice Whisper's word count, or one 4-gram four or more times); runs of three or more consecutive words that only Nemotron has (possible inventions); runs of three or more that only Whisper has (possible losses); each run marked as next to a pause of 1 s or more or not, by the energy scan used for this brief.
3. `[check]` `adapt`: the 114 padded twins at the -50 dBFS level (the level of the owner's live noise floor; the runtime skips audio below -60 dBFS peak, so this level reaches the model) are decoded in N0 and N2 and paired with the Stage 2 decode of the same clip without the pad. Reported per arm and language: twins whose decode gained words after the last word of the unpadded decode; twins that lost trailing words; loops; and the same two counts for Whisper from the October run's untrimmed decodes of the same twins.
4. `[judge]` `new`: every run from criterion 2 (both directions, both arms) is settled by ear, because neither engine is a reference for the other: the owner gets one local page with a play button at the right second and the two texts side by side, and marks each as "Nemotron invented", "Nemotron lost speech", or "Whisper was wrong". Capped at 20 runs, longest first; with zero runs there is nothing to judge. The verdict is read from the count of confirmed inventions and confirmed losses in arm N2.
5. `[check]` `new`: the verdict is computed from the frozen table and written, with the counts, to the slice's evidence and to spike 020's results file, and the spike's memory and item records say how it ended. The table (the owner chose "Strict" on 2026-11-14): PASS when arm N2 has no loop on either sample, no confirmed invention and no confirmed loss on the real recordings, and on the twins no more clips with gained or lost trailing words than Whisper has on the same twins; anything else is FAIL. Arm N0 is reported and decides nothing.
6. `[check]` `exists`: no audio and no text leaves the Mac during the run (`lsof` socket sampling as in Stages 1 and 2, log empty), and no dictation text enters a tracked file (the evidence holds counts and file hashes only).

**Not in this slice.** Building the engine or any app change. Sizing the whole-dictation fabrication seen in Stage 2: one event in 114 clips cannot be sized by a sample of this size, and this slice only reports whether it recurs. Leading and interior pauses built artificially (the real recordings supply them). The -56 dBFS twin level. iOS. Deleting the 21 GB of model files after a FAIL (asked separately).

**Constraints.** Privacy (CLAUDE.md): "all processing happens on-device; no audio or text goes to any server", and "Dictation samples from the debug logs never enter a tracked file": decodes, recordings and the listening page stay in the gitignored planning tree and the spike's cache. The usage fact: any engine "must leave internal and leading pauses untouched and must never drop speech that follows a pause". Host rules: the run starts through the resource gate, on mains power, one model at a time; Stage 2's watchdog stays (abort on swap growth above 8 GB, footprint above 26 GB); the swap guard aborted three Stage 2 attempts when other sessions started jobs, and decoding is deterministic, so a partial run may be resumed. Expected compute: about 3,400 s of twin audio (the 2,761 s of Stage 2 plus 3 to 8 s of pad on each of 114 clips) and 739 s of real audio per arm at the measured 8 to 9 times real time, about 16 minutes of decoding for both arms plus six model loads; the plan names the ceiling and asks for it. No dependency on another project.

**Size and risk.** Quick: three tasks (freeze and preflight, decode, score and report), one session, no new dependency, no code in the app. Risk: none. The plan is asked apart from this brief because the run has a ceiling.

**Open.** Whether a PASS leads to an engine slice: a PASS creates a planned item for the owner to schedule, nothing more. Whether a larger real sample (a full week of retained audio) should repeat the measurement before any engine work.
