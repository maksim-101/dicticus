# 62 nemotron-stage3-pause-phantoms-spike (quick)

## Brief

**Outcome.** The owner can decide, from counted results, whether Nemotron 3.5 Transcribe is safe to offer as an opt-in macOS engine given how the owner dictates: the number of dictations in which it invents words in or after a thinking pause, and the number in which it loses speech that follows a pause, measured on real dictations with pauses and on the padded test clips, next to Whisper's figures on the same audio. Spike 020 then ends with a verdict (PASS or FAIL by a rule frozen before the first decode) in place of an open Stage 3.

**Problem.** Spike 020 is two thirds done and its last question is the one that decides it. Stage 1 passed (runs on Metal, 19.5 GB footprint, 1.2 s for a 10 s clip). Stage 2 passed (German 3.74 against Whisper's 5.35 word errors per 100 words, English a tie, dictionary terms help English), but under the plain prompt one of 114 dictations (136 s long) came back as 198 invented words. Nothing has been measured on pauses, and the owner holds the hotkey through pauses of one to several seconds at the start, in the middle and at the end of a dictation. Checked on 2026-11-14 for this brief:

- The October pause clips (quick 261018-940) cover trailing pauses only: 118 clips with 3 to 8 s of room tone appended, at two noise levels. They hold no leading and no interior pause. Whisper produced 0 phantoms on the 82 single-window clips of that set while it does produce phantoms live, so a zero there says little by itself.
- Since slice 60 (this morning) the debug build keeps each dictation's audio. 33 recordings exist (739 s in total, median 18 s, longest 74 s); 28 of them hold a pause of 1 s or more: 10 leading, 20 interior, 7 trailing, the longest 11 s, 15 s and 18 s. These are the pauses the usage fact describes, and the first sample on which a pause phantom can show.
- The model files (19.7 GB and 1.7 GB), the runtime build (`nemo-speech 0.2.0` starts) and the Stage 2 decodes of the unpadded clips (arms N0 and N2) are on disk.

**For whom.** The owner, deciding whether a later slice builds the opt-in engine for Macs with 32 GB or more; nobody else uses the result.

**Done criteria.**

1. `[check]` `new`: the rule is frozen before any Stage 3 decode exists: sample lists (file hashes), arms, scoring rules and the PASS/FAIL table, hash-locked as in Stage 2, and the scorer refuses to run when a hash differs. Observed by the file times (the lock file is older than the first decode output) and by the scorer's hash check.
2. `[check]` `new`: the real-recording sample is decoded by Nemotron in two arms, N0 (the runtime's plain prompt) and N2 (the same prompt plus the 182 dictionary terms frozen in Stage 2, which is what a product would send), with the language each dictation's debug record names. The sample is every retained recording that exists at the freeze, copied to the spike's cache outside the repo so the seven-day purge cannot change it. Compared word by word with the Whisper text the app produced for the same audio (the record's raw transcript). Reported per arm, as counts: generation loops (Stage 2's rule: more than twice Whisper's word count, or one 4-gram four or more times); runs of three or more consecutive words that only Nemotron has (possible inventions); runs of three or more that only Whisper has (possible losses); each run marked as next to a pause of 1 s or more or not, by the energy scan used for this brief.
3. `[check]` `adapt`: the 114 padded twins at the -50 dBFS level (the level of the owner's live noise floor; the runtime skips audio below -60 dBFS peak, so this level reaches the model) are decoded in N0 and N2 and paired with the Stage 2 decode of the same clip without the pad. Reported per arm and language: twins whose decode gained words after the last word of the unpadded decode; twins that lost trailing words; loops; and the same two counts for Whisper from the October run's untrimmed decodes of the same twins.
4. `[judge]` `new`: every run from criterion 2 (both directions, both arms) is settled by ear, because neither engine is a reference for the other: the owner gets one local page with a play button at the right second and the two texts side by side, and marks each as "Nemotron invented", "Nemotron lost speech", or "Whisper was wrong". Capped at 20 runs, longest first; with zero runs there is nothing to judge. The verdict is read from the count of confirmed inventions and confirmed losses in arm N2.
5. `[check]` `new`: the verdict is computed from the frozen table and written, with the counts, to the slice's evidence and to spike 020's results file, and the spike's memory and item records say how it ended. The table (the owner chose "Strict" on 2026-11-14): PASS when arm N2 has no loop on either sample, no confirmed invention and no confirmed loss on the real recordings, and on the twins no more clips with gained or lost trailing words than Whisper has on the same twins; anything else is FAIL. Arm N0 is reported and decides nothing.
6. `[check]` `exists`: no audio and no text leaves the Mac during the run (`lsof` socket sampling as in Stages 1 and 2, log empty), and no dictation text enters a tracked file (the evidence holds counts and file hashes only).

**Not in this slice.** Building the engine or any app change. Sizing the whole-dictation fabrication seen in Stage 2: one event in 114 clips cannot be sized by a sample of this size, and this slice only reports whether it recurs. Leading and interior pauses built artificially (the real recordings supply them). The -56 dBFS twin level. iOS. Deleting the 21 GB of model files after a FAIL (asked separately).

**Constraints.** Privacy (CLAUDE.md): "all processing happens on-device; no audio or text goes to any server", and "Dictation samples from the debug logs never enter a tracked file": decodes, recordings and the listening page stay in the gitignored planning tree and the spike's cache. The usage fact: any engine "must leave internal and leading pauses untouched and must never drop speech that follows a pause". Host rules: the run starts through the resource gate, on mains power, one model at a time; Stage 2's watchdog stays (abort on swap growth above 8 GB, footprint above 26 GB); the swap guard aborted three Stage 2 attempts when other sessions started jobs, and decoding is deterministic, so a partial run may be resumed. Expected compute: about 3,400 s of twin audio (the 2,761 s of Stage 2 plus 3 to 8 s of pad on each of 114 clips) and 739 s of real audio per arm at the measured 8 to 9 times real time, about 16 minutes of decoding for both arms plus six model loads; the plan names the ceiling and asks for it. No dependency on another project.

**Size and risk.** Quick: three tasks (freeze and preflight, decode, score and report), one session, no new dependency, no code in the app. Risk: none. The plan is asked apart from this brief because the run has a ceiling.

**Open.** Whether a PASS leads to an engine slice: a PASS creates a planned item for the owner to schedule, nothing more. Whether a larger real sample (a full week of retained audio) should repeat the measurement before any engine work.

## Plan

**Route.** The sample: the owner's real recordings plus the -50 dBFS trailing twins (chosen) against building leading and interior pauses artificially into the corpus clips (rejected: Whisper showed 0 of 82 phantoms on artificial room tone while it does produce them live, and 28 of the 33 real recordings already hold a real pause in all three positions). Settling a disputed passage: the owner's ear, at most 20 passages (chosen) against a third engine as referee (rejected: Parakeet has its own errors, so its vote would decide nothing, and it would need a new driver). The twin comparison reuses two earlier results, each compared only with its own kind: Nemotron's Stage 2 decodes of the unpadded clips (same binary `nemo-speech 0.2.0` at 66d50d9, same model files by hash, same config and instruction strings; shown comparable by decoding three unpadded clips per language and arm again inside the Stage 3 batches and requiring byte-identical text), and Whisper's October decodes of the same twins and originals (arms `A` and `C-sil50` of quick 261018-940, 118 rows each, one driver, one run). The earliest cheap signal is the first batch (N0, German, the smallest): its time gives the projection, and its three re-decoded originals show whether the Stage 2 pairing holds. Fragile assumption: Whisper's shipped raw text is close enough to Nemotron's wording that a run of three or more differing words marks a real disagreement and not style; if the scorer finds more than 20 runs, the measure is too noisy to hand over, and the slice stops for the owner before any listening.

All spike files (scripts, lists, decodes, the listening page) live in the gitignored planning tree under `.planning/spikes/020-nemotron-transcribe-macos/stage3/` and the spike's cache outside the repo; `.flow/62-nemotron-stage3-pause-phantoms-spike/evidence/` gets counts, hashes and logs without dictation text.

### Task 1: freeze the sample and the rule, preflight
- **Goal:** the real-recording sample is a fixed copy (every WAV in the debug build's audio folder that has a log record, with the record's language and Whisper raw text beside it, in the spike's cache); the lists, the arms, the scoring rules and the PASS/FAIL table of criterion 5 are written in `STAGE3-PREREG.md` and `stage3-thresholds.json` and hash-locked in `STAGE3-LOCK.sha256`; the scorer exists and its self-test passes; the model hashes and the runtime are confirmed. The scoring rules fixed here: Stage 2's normaliser and filler strip on every text; the loop rule; a run is three or more consecutive words present in one engine's text only after alignment; a run is "at a pause" when the energy scan of this brief finds 1 s or more of silence in the recording (leading, interior or trailing); on the twins, gained and lost trailing words by the rules of `scoring_940.py`; Whisper's twin count is computed from the October decodes and written into the freeze, since it reads no Stage 3 decode. More than 20 runs in total stops the slice for the owner.
- **Files:** new under the planning tree: `stage3/STAGE3-PREREG.md`, `stage3/stage3-thresholds.json`, `stage3/STAGE3-LOCK.sha256`, `stage3/stage3_freeze.py`, `stage3/stage3_score.py`; the sample copy in the spike's cache; `evidence/freeze.md` (counts per language and pause position, hashes of the frozen files, Whisper's twin counts).
- **Affects:** nothing in the app or the repo's tracked code. The copy keeps recordings beyond the debug build's seven-day purge, on this Mac only; it is deleted when spike 020 closes or the engine item ends.
- **Verify:** `shasum -a 256 -c STAGE3-LOCK.sha256` prints OK for every line; `uv run --no-project python -I stage3_score.py --self-test` prints `self-test: all passed` (synthetic strings: a loop, a three-word run in each direction, a two-word run that must not count, a gained and a lost trailing word) and exits non-zero on a failing assertion, seen failing once with the run length set to 2; `shasum -a 256 -c expected.sha256` in the cache's model folder prints OK for both files.
- **Status:** todo
- **Depends on:** none

### Task 2: the decode run
- **Goal:** four batches (N0 and N2, German and English), each one model load over that language's twins, three unpadded originals and real recordings, with JSON output per clip.
- **Files:** new `stage3/stage3-run.sh` (Stage 2's script with its watchdog: abort on swap growth above 8 GB, footprint above 26 GB or 45 minutes of wall time; socket sampling; plus `pmset -g batt` and thermal state per sample); outputs under `stage3/<arm>-<lang>/`; `evidence/run.md` (the watch log's summary lines, power and thermal summary, socket log size).
- **Affects:** the Mac while it runs: about 19.5 GB of memory and the GPU, so other apps may go to swap and the owner's AI cleanup may be slow or time out for about 20 minutes (the 2026-11-10 audit traced cleanup timeouts to another model on the GPU). Starts through `heavy-gate acquire nemotron-stage3 --class model --mem 21 --wait 1800`, calls `heavy-gate check` between batches and pauses on exit 10, releases at the end. Mains power only.
- **Run size:** expected about 18 minutes of decoding (about 4,200 s of audio per arm at the 8 to 9 times real time measured in Stages 1 and 2, plus four loads); ceiling 27 minutes of decoding, gate waits not counted. The first batch (N0, German) is the timed sample: if its rate projects the whole run past 27 minutes, the run stops there and the owner is asked. Decoding is deterministic, so an aborted batch is resumed, not repeated from the start.
- **Verify:** `stage3-run.sh` exits 0 and its last line reads `total_secs=…`; every batch line shows `rc=0 reason=ok` and `files=` equal to the input count; `sockets.log` is empty; every power sample reads `AC Power`; `stage3_score.py --check-pairing` prints `12 of 12 identical` for the re-decoded originals against Stage 2 and exits non-zero otherwise.
- **Status:** todo
- **Depends on:** task 1

### Task 3: score, the listening page, the report
- **Goal:** `stage3/result.json` holds the counts of criteria 2 and 3 per arm; when runs exist (at most 20), a local page lists each with a player set to its second, both texts, and three buttons, and shows one line of answers to copy back; with no runs, or once the owner's answers are in, the verdict is computed from the frozen table and written to `STAGE3-RESULTS.md` in the planning tree, `evidence/result.md` (counts and verdict only), the spike's memory note and the item.
- **Files:** `stage3/result.json`, `stage3/listen.html`, `STAGE3-RESULTS.md`, `evidence/result.md`, `evidence/listen-render.png` only if the page shows no dictation text in the shot (otherwise a text note of the render check).
- **Affects:** nothing tracked beyond the evidence; a PASS adds one planned item (the opt-in engine) for the owner to schedule, a FAIL closes spike 020 in its records.
- **Verify:** `uv run --no-project python -I stage3_score.py` exits 0 after confirming the lock hashes and prints the counts and either `verdict: PASS`, `verdict: FAIL` or `verdict: pending N passages`; the page is rendered headless and each player's start second and button count read back; `grep -c` for any word of the decodes in `.flow/62-nemotron-stage3-pause-phantoms-spike/evidence/` is covered by the evidence files holding numbers and hashes only (checked by reading them).
- **Status:** todo
- **Depends on:** task 2

**Criteria to tasks.** 1 → task 1 (lock and scorer check), with the file-time order read in task 2's evidence. 2 → tasks 2 and 3. 3 → tasks 1 (Whisper's counts), 2 and 3. 4 `[judge]` → task 3 builds the page; the owner's answers are taken at verify. 5 → task 3. 6 → task 2 (sockets) and task 3 (evidence content).
