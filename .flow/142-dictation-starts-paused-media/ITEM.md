# A dictation never starts playback that the owner had paused

The owner, 2026-11-21: for about two weeks, now and then, after a video or music had been playing and the owner stopped it by hand, a later dictation (sometimes about three dictations later), with no audio playing at all, starts playback while dictating. A bug to investigate. Likely area: the media auto-pause's MediaRemote toggle path (tier 2b), which sends a play/pause toggle rather than an explicit pause and resumes with a second toggle; the 2026-11-21 audit counted 5 tier-2b events with a resume toggle in 11 days (`.planning/debug/audit-2026-11-21/lens-fixes.md`, media section). Logs: `mediapause-*.jsonl` in the debug recordings.

Investigation 2026-11-21 (read-only agent, `.planning/debug/audit-2026-11-21/media-autoplay.md`; the seven PLAY timestamps and the code lines checked by me): tier 2b sends a discrete PAUSE at key-down and a discrete PLAY at release (`MediaController.swift:707-722`), decided by one ~100 ms output-RMS sample above 1e-3 (`tier2bShouldFire`, line 310) that does not know which app made the sound. Seven release PLAYs since 11-14 (11-14, 11-16, 11-17, 11-20 ×2, 11-21 ×2), none 11-01..13. Four fit a short non-media sound at key-down (RMS 0.0027-0.0034, below the ~9.2e-3 the code's comment gives for playback) that ended by itself, which the verify step reads as a successful pause; one PLAY went out unverified because the key was released before verification. The PLAY then reaches the app that still holds media control (a paused video or Music), which explains "only now and then" and "several dictations later". Open: the owner reports playback starting while dictating; the code can only send PLAY at release. Deciding observation at the next incident: the time and whether the key was still held. Fix direction (not built): send PLAY only after a completed `stopped` verification, and send PAUSE only when a second sample ~0.3 s later is still above the threshold; two regression tests (weak short sound; early release) that fail today; `testEarlyRelease_CancelsVerificationBeforeSideEffects` and `testVerifyUnmeasurable_TreatedAsStopped` expectations change.

Reproduced 2026-11-21 in mac-space (Debug-Recorder build of branch flow/52-dictionary-ux, media pause on, evidence in `evidence/`): QuickTime played a spoken file and was paused by a click; then a short spoken notice played through `afplay` (not a media app) and the dictation hotkey was tapped (press and release within about 15 ms, as cua can only tap). Log `evidence/vm-mediapause-2026-11-21.jsonl`: in each of three taps the press sampled RMS 0.063-0.079, sent the MediaRemote toggle at press, sent the resume toggle at release, and the verification ended `cancelled`. QuickTime was playing afterwards in run 1 (paused 0:05, playing 0:25: `vm-run1-before-paused.png`, `vm-run1-after-playing.png`) and run 3 (0:14 paused, 0:20 playing); run 2's before-shot shows QuickTime already playing, so it is inconclusive. A control tap with no sound (RMS 0) sent nothing. This confirms the early-release path with a non-media sound; the held-key path (the owner's "while dictating") was not run, because the VM tools cannot hold a key.

Held-key runs 2026-11-21 13:58-13:59 local (guest tool `keyhold`, posting Ctrl+Shift+S down, holding, up; started by a one-shot LaunchAgent so TCC attributes it to the tool, Post Event granted by the owner in the guest): (1) a 3.7 s spoken notice playing at the press, 6 s hold: press RMS 0.018, pause sent, verify `still_playing` (the notice was still sounding), no resume at release, QuickTime stayed paused at 0:37 (`vm-hold1-*.png`, `vm-hold1-log.txt`): no defect. (2) a 0.45 s "okay" at the press, 4 s hold: press RMS 0.10, pause sent, verify `stopped` 0.46 s later (the sound had ended by itself), resume sent at release, QuickTime went from paused at 0:37 (before and during the hold) to playing at 0:40 after release (`vm-hold2-*.png`, `vm-hold2-log.txt`). This is the main path the investigation named (four of seven live incidents). Playback starts at release, not during the hold; the owner's "while dictating" fits the moment right after release while the text is still being produced (inferred). Control: a 0.3 s tap with no sound sent nothing. Both paths that start paused media are now reproduced: early release (verify cancelled) and a short sound that ends before the verify (verify stopped).
