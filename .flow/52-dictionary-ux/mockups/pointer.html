Board 1 of 2: after a misheard word, how should Dicticus lead you to the dictionary?

Question: after a dictation with a misheard word, how should Dicticus lead you to the dictionary? Today the only route is popover, History tab, Open Full History…, expand the row by its unlabelled chevron, click the word, Add to Dictionary… (about 7 clicks).

Direction: native macOS Sonoma/Tahoe window and popover, system font, dense list; signature element: the heard-as chips. Each variant is drawn in its real frame (popover open under the menu bar on the desktop) as a short sequence. Red numbers are clicks, counted from the open popover. Anything the app does not have yet carries NEW.

Example: the last dictation heard “mirror cove” where you meant Miracove (invented names only). The same dictation is used in all three rows.

A — “Fix a word…” button on Home

Differs in: the Home card gets a third button; it opens the History window with that dictation already expanded, Raw selected and a hint above the words.

Cost: small (a button plus one call that opens History with an entry preselected). Limit: only the last dictation. Clicks: 2 from the open popover to the pick (Fix a word…, then the word).

B — pick the word on the Home card itself

Differs in: no second window. A Polished | Raw switch on the card shows the raw words; each word lights up on hover and a click opens the Add to Dictionary sheet with “Heard as” filled in.

Cost: medium (a new word-pick control inside the popover: wrapping words, hover state, selection). Limit: only the last dictation. Clicks: 2 (Raw, then the word); 1 if the card opened on Raw by default.

C — “Fix” button on every History row

Differs in: each row of the popover’s History tab gets a small Fix button beside Copy; it opens the History window with that row expanded on Raw.

Cost: small (one button per row, same call as A). Limit: needs the History tab first, so one click more than A. Clicks: 3 (History tab, Fix, the word). Gain: reaches older dictations, not just the last one.
