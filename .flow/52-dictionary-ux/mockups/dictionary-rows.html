Board 1 of 2 — how one correct spelling and its misheard forms look in the Manage Dictionary window

Direction: the existing Manage Dictionary window (macOS DictionaryView): native inset table, 13 pt system text, ultra-thin toolbar and footer, accent green only for the primary button and the selection. Signature element: the misheard forms shown on the row of the spelling they belong to. Only the list area differs between the variants; toolbar, Starter Packs and Spoken Punctuation sections below the footer stay as they are (not drawn).

The data: invented example words; the header counts are your dictionary's real ones (306 entries, 183 correct spellings). Anything the app does not have today is marked NEW. Toolbar labels, the sort picker (Recent / A–Z) and the footer buttons are the app's own; the footer keeps Delete Selected, Remove All, Export and Import.

Common to all three: the row shows the correct spelling once; adding a misheard form, removing one, changing the spelling (for all its forms at once) and deleting the row are available. A misheard form that is an everyday word is outlined orange, because it rewrites that word everywhere (here: "cloud").

A — table with chips

Two columns, spelling first. Misheard forms are small chips on the row, each with its own ×; a dashed "+" chip adds one. Double-click the spelling to rename it. Closest to today's table.
