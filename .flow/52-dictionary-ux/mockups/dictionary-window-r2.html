Board 2 of 2, round 2: where do Spoken Punctuation, Voice Commands and Starter Packs live?

Question: where does the search field go, and where do the reference sections go so the list keeps its room?

What changed since round 1: the owner liked the search row above the list but wanted it “more tidy and modern”, and rejected the bottom panel and the reference popover (“not a fan of half split screens or pop-ups that don’t seem to be able to be closed”). The Case Sensitive toggle is removed. So in every variant the search is a rounded field with a magnifier, a clear button once text is typed, and ⌘F, in one header band with the count and the Recent | A–Z control. The variants differ only in where the three reference areas live, each fully visible and never a split or a floating popup.

Direction: native macOS Sonoma/Tahoe window, system font, dense list; signature element: the heard-as chips. Anything the app does not have yet carries NEW. Common to all: column headers “Correct spelling” and “Heard as”, one explanation line under the band. The Settings look in C follows the app’s own Settings window (icon tabs Hotkeys, AI Cleanup, General, About). Invented words only.

A — Sidebar

Differs in: a sidebar inside the dictionary window (Entries, Spoken Punctuation, Voice Commands, Starter Packs). Choosing an item swaps the whole content pane, so each page is fully visible and scrolls by itself; nothing floats and nothing splits the window.

Cost: large (sidebar navigation container, four panes, the footer moves into the Entries pane). Room for the list: pane is 570 of 720 px wide but the full height, about 10 rows (34 px each). Entries show the search band and the footer; the other panes have neither.

B — Tabs in the title bar

Differs in: three tabs at the top of the window (Entries | Spoken Punctuation | Voice Commands) switch the whole window. Starter Packs move into an “Add ▾” menu in the footer next to Edit (Add Entry…, Import CSV…, Starter Packs ▸ Tech Terms / Brand Names / General Terms), and Import leaves the footer.

Cost: small to medium (tab control, one menu, two reference panes). Room for the list: full width and about 10 rows; the title bar grows by about 16 px.

C — Spoken commands in Settings

Differs in: the dictionary window keeps only entries: search band, list, footer. Spoken Punctuation and Voice Commands become the tab “Spoken Commands” in the existing Settings window (Hotkeys, AI Cleanup, General, About), reached by a “Spoken commands…” link in the dictionary footer. Starter Packs sit in the “Add ▾” menu as in B.

Cost: small to medium (one Settings tab, a footer link that opens it, the Add menu). Room for the list: full window, about 10 rows. Reference lives where other settings live, so it is not part of the list task at all.
